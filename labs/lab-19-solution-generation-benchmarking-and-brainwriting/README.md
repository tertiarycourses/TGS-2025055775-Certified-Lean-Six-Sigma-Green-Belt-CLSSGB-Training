# Lab 19 — Solution Generation, Benchmarking and Brainwriting

**DMAIC phase:** IMPROVE  |  **Lab type:** Core  |  **Course:** Certified Lean Six Sigma Green Belt (CLSSGB) Training (TGS-2025055775)

## Objective

Generate a wide solution set against the proven root causes (A5).

## Scenario

Northwind Retail Distribution Centre fulfils online orders from a 12,000 sqm warehouse. Customers are complaining about late deliveries and the company is receiving negative feedback on social media. The Distribution Manager has asked you, as Green Belt, to lead a DMAIC project on the order-fulfilment process — from order release to carrier handover.

## What you will build

A solution log of at least fifteen candidate solutions mapped to proven root causes.

**Tools and techniques:** Brainstorming, brainwriting, six thinking hats, anti-brainstorming, benchmarking, SCAMPER

## Data for this lab

Open the workbook(s) below from this lab's `data/` folder. Every lab uses the same Northwind baseline month, so the figures reconcile across the whole course.

- **[`lab19-improvement-ideas.xlsx`](data/lab19-improvement-ideas.xlsx)** — Ideas generated by the team's brainwriting session plus external benchmark findings, ready to be screened in Lab 21.

**Working with `lab19-improvement-ideas.xlsx`:**

- I-09 is a solution looking for a problem — note why it fails a cost-benefit screen.
- Each idea should trace back to a cause you PROVED in Labs 15-18, not to a hunch.

## Steps

### Step 1

List your validated root causes from Labs 17 and 18, then open lab19-improvement-ideas.xlsx — the team's ideas and benchmark findings, each tagged to the cause it targets. Solutions without a cause are pet projects.

### Step 2

Run a structured brainstorm with the ground rules displayed: no criticism during generation, quantity over quality, build on others' ideas, wild ideas welcome.

### Step 3

Run brainwriting for the quieter participants: each person writes three ideas silently, then passes the sheet on for others to build upon.

### Step 4

Apply anti-brainstorming: ask how you could make the problem WORSE, then invert each answer into an improvement.

### Step 5

Use six thinking hats to examine the leading ideas from different perspectives — facts, feelings, risks, benefits, creativity and process.

### Step 6

Benchmark: identify who performs this process best, internally or in another industry, and record what they do differently.

### Step 7

Follow the four benchmarking steps — plan what to benchmark, collect the comparison data, analyse the gap, and adapt rather than copy.

### Step 8

Map every generated solution against the root cause it addresses in a two-column table, and discard any that address no proven cause.

### Step 9

Group similar solutions and remove exact duplicates, keeping at least fifteen distinct candidates.

## Check your work

You have at least fifteen distinct solutions, every one traces to a root cause proven with data, and at least three came from benchmarking.

## Deliverable

Save your output — it forms part of your Northwind improvement package and is your revision material for the assessment.
