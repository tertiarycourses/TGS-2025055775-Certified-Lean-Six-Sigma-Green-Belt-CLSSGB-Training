# Labs — Certified Lean Six Sigma Green Belt (CLSSGB) Training

**WSQ Course Code:** TGS-2025055775  |  **Version v5 · 23 September 2026**

These labs follow the DMAIC roadmap end to end. Every lab builds on the same Northwind Retail Distribution Centre scenario, so your outputs accumulate into one complete improvement package.

## How the labs are organised

Each lab has its own folder containing the lab sheet (`README.md`) and a `data/` folder with the Excel workbook(s) that lab works on:

```
labs/
  lab-01-the-green-belt-role.../
    README.md        the lab sheet
    data/*.xlsx      the mock data for this lab
```

All the data is one reconciled month of Northwind order-fulfilment records — **4,200 orders, 357 of them late (8.5%)** — the same baseline quoted in the assessment. Your Lab 2 sigma level, your Lab 15 Pareto and your Lab 25 before/after all come from that one dataset, so the numbers agree end to end.

## Lab types

- **Core** — completed by everyone; maps directly to the assessment.
- **Elective** — additional practice with further Lean Six Sigma tools; run when time allows or after the course.
